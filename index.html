Oopbuy shopping guide

From saved link
to considered choice.

Use the same four checks for every listing. Consistent notes make different products easier to assess.

01

Open the source listing

Check that the link still opens the intended product. Confirm the selected color, size and variant instead of relying on a spreadsheet thumbnail.

02

Measure something you own

Use a well-fitting garment or shoe as your reference. Record the actual measurements and units beside the listing's size chart.

03

Read the visual details

Compare front, back and close-up photographs. Look for stitching, fastenings, material texture and any unclear or missing details.

04

Estimate the whole cost

Add item price, domestic delivery, applicable fees, international shipping and relevant taxes or duties. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Clear answers to common spreadsheet and product-comparison questions.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping ideas and comparison notes. It may include categories, prices, sizes and links. Each entry still needs to be checked against its source listing.

Is this an official Oopbuy website?

No. This is an independent fashion discovery guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS. It does not claim an official relationship with Oopbuy or the brands discussed.

Can I download a spreadsheet here?

This page provides a category directory and buying checklist rather than a downloadable spreadsheet. Use the category links to browse CAFANS and build your own comparison list.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing's measurements with an item you already own. Check measurement units, foot length for shoes, and chest, length, waist or inseam measurements for clothing. A familiar size label alone does not establish fit.

Does a spreadsheet prove product authenticity or availability?

No. A spreadsheet entry or product photograph does not establish authenticity, stock or seller authorization. Verify the source listing, seller information and any supporting documentation separately.

What costs should I record before ordering?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, any applicable service fees, international shipping estimate and applicable taxes or duties. Estimates can change when the final package weight, dimensions and destination are known.
